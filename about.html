Our story

WebSecretaries.com was founded in the 1990s out of pure necessity. Our founders were juggling too many tasks, competing priorities and real-life events to get done everything they needed to get done. They knew they would benefit from secretarial help, but could not justify a full-time administrative assistant.

WebSecretaries.com was started to give busy entrepreneurs, home-office workers, small business owners, students and even over-worked stay-at-home parents the help they need, when they need it, through affordable personal assistant services.

Being virtually delivered and personally managed means you get individual attention, and an individual WebSecretary to work with.
