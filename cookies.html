The short version

WebSecretaries.com does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and does not set any cookies of its own.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They are often used to remember logins, preferences or browsing activity.

Third-party services on this site

A few third-party services help the Site work. They may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, when your browser connects to them:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the Site and may log technical data for security and operations.
  • Google Fonts supplies the Site's typefaces. Google states that it does not set cookies for Google Fonts.
  • Basin processes our online request form when you submit it, and may use cookies or similar technologies needed for spam protection.

These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. The Site will continue to work without them, although our request form may ask you to complete an extra spam check.

Changes

If we ever add cookies, for example for visitor statistics, we will update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
